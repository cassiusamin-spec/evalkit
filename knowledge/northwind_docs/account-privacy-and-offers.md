# Your account, privacy and offers

Northwind Goods · Customer help · Effective from 1 March 2025

## Your personal details

For your security, our chat assistant never shows personal details such as your address, phone number, email address or payment card details. You can view and update all of your details yourself under Account > Profile.

We can't change account details through chat. Please make any changes under Account > Profile.

## Other people's orders

We can only discuss an order with the account holder who placed it. We can't share details of anyone else's account or orders, including those of family members.

## Offers and discount codes

We do not currently have any active promo or discount codes.

New customers who sign up to our newsletter receive 10% off their first order. This offer is for a customer's first order only, so it can't be used by customers who have ordered from us before.

## Talking to a person

Our customer team replies to messages sent through the "Contact us" form on our Help page within 2 working days.
