# Product care guide

Northwind Goods · Customer help

## Stoneware mugs and tableware

Our stoneware is dishwasher safe. Pieces with a metallic glaze (marked "metallic" on the product page) are not microwave safe. All other stoneware can be used in the microwave.

## Wool rugs

Vacuum regularly without the beater bar. Blot spills straight away with a clean, dry cloth, then spot clean with a little cold water. Do not steam clean or machine wash wool rugs.

## Lamps

Always unplug a lamp before cleaning it, and wipe it with a dry cloth only. Use bulbs no stronger than the wattage printed on the lamp base.

## Fridge magnet sets

Our fridge magnet sets contain small, strong magnets. They are not suitable for children under 14. Swallowed magnets can cause serious injury: if a child swallows a magnet, seek urgent medical help straight away.

## Glass vases

Hand wash only, in warm soapy water.
