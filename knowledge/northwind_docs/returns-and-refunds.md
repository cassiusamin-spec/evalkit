# Returns and refunds

Northwind Goods · Customer help · Effective from 1 March 2025
This policy replaces all earlier returns policies.

## Your 30-day refund window

You can return any item within 30 days of the date you receive it for a full refund to your original payment method.

To get a refund you need a receipt. Your order confirmation email counts as a receipt.

## After 30 days

After 30 days we can no longer offer a refund. Instead, we can give you store credit for the value of the item, as long as you still have a receipt. Store credit can be used on any future order.

## How to return an item

1. Sign in and go to Account > Orders.
2. Choose the order and select "Return an item".
3. Print the returns label and attach it to the parcel.
4. Drop the parcel off at any post office.

Please return items in their original packaging where possible.

## Damaged or broken on arrival

If something arrives damaged, please contact us through the "Contact us" form on our Help page within 30 days of delivery, with a photo of the damage. Damaged items returned within 30 days are refunded in full.

## Gifts

If you received an item as a gift, the 30-day window starts from the date the gift was delivered. You will need the gift receipt or the order confirmation email from the person who bought it.
