# Delivery and shipping

Northwind Goods · Customer help · Effective from 1 March 2025

## Where we deliver

We deliver to five countries only:

- United Kingdom (including Northern Ireland)
- Ireland
- France
- Germany
- Spain

We are not able to deliver anywhere else at the moment, including other EU countries.

## Delivery times

| Service | Delivery time |
|---|---|
| Standard | 3-5 working days |
| Express | Next working day, if you order before 2pm |

Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Express orders placed after 2pm are treated as placed on the next working day.

## Tracking your order

When your order is dispatched we send you an email with a tracking link. If your tracking link has not updated for several days, or your order is later than the times above, please contact us through the "Contact us" form on our Help page and we will look into it.

## If you're not in when we deliver

Our couriers will leave a card with instructions for redelivery or collection.
