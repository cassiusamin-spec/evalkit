Score the tone of a reply to an upset customer.

A good reply: acknowledges the problem, apologises sincerely without grovelling,
states a concrete next step with a time frame, and avoids blaming the customer.

5 = all four present, natural and concise.
4 = all present, slightly stiff or generic.
3 = missing the concrete next step or the time frame.
2 = dismissive, or apology without any action.
1 = rude, blames the customer, or ignores the complaint.
